HabitFlow Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 29, 2026

Thank you for using HabitFlow. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how HabitFlow collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

HabitFlow is designed to respect your privacy. Most of your habit information is stored locally on your device. The application does not require you to create an account before using its core features.

Depending on how you use the app, HabitFlow may process:

  • Habit names and descriptions
  • Habit completion history
  • Reminder preferences
  • Application settings
  • Progress statistics

2. Personal Information

HabitFlow does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, email address, or home address unless you voluntarily contact us for support.

3. Data Storage

Your habit data is primarily stored locally on your device. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal habit information with third parties.

4. Permissions Used

HabitFlow may request certain Android permissions in order to provide its intended functionality. These permissions may include:

  • Notifications – to remind you to complete your habits.
  • Vibration – to provide reminder feedback.
  • Wake Lock – to ensure reminder notifications function correctly.
  • Storage Access (if applicable) – only when required for features such as sharing or exporting information initiated by the user.

These permissions are only used for the purposes described above.

5. Third-Party Services

HabitFlow may use trusted third-party libraries and platform services that are necessary for application functionality. These services operate according to their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

HabitFlow is intended for general audiences and is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page together with the updated effective date.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request information, correction, or deletion where applicable. If you have any privacy-related questions, please contact us.
